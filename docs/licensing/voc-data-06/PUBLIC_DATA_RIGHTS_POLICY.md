**Public distribution conditions if eventually signed:**

1. Preserve NGSL authors' names, dataset source, CC BY-SA 4.0 link and changes notice; if any published portion is protected adapted material, comply with its ShareAlike conditions. Do not claim the whole dataset or app is automatically CC BY-SA.
2. Obtain an authorized, written public redistribution permission for the project-editorial parts, **to the extent rights exist and can be licensed**. Do not fabricate an author, copyright claim or CC license solely from an AI-generation label or acceptance signature.
3. Identify exact scope, channels (public repository, archive, APK, store), approved snapshot/hash and release date, and record the notices distributed with those bytes.
4. Review provenance/rights separately for each new source and snapshot; do not silently rewrite frozen `accepted-v1`. Create a new versioned handoff after approval.
5. Make attribution accessible offline in Android and test release paths, including DATA-08 upgrades/rollback.
